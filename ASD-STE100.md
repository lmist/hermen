# Hermen

## 1 Purpose

Hermen helps a person get ready for a therapy session. The person talks with Hermen before the session. Hermen helps the person find the right words. The person takes a one-page prep card to the session.

## 2 Users

The user is a person who plans to attend therapy. The user can also be a person who is on a waiting list. The user can be a person who has not started therapy yet.

Hermen has two guides. Hermen is the male guide. Herma is the female guide. Both guides are Masky avatars. The user chooses one guide.

## 3 Function

Hermen does four things. Hermen reflects back what the user said. Hermen flags words that a therapist could misread. Hermen helps the user say the hardest thing three ways. Hermen makes a prep card.

The prep card has five parts. The parts are: what the user wants the therapist to understand, the hardest thing in three ways, words that might be misread, what the user wants from the session, and one check-back question.

Hermen asks one question at a time. Hermen uses the words of the user. Hermen does not use jargon.

## 4 Limits

Hermen is not a therapist. Hermen does not diagnose. Hermen does not give medical advice. Hermen does not replace a human therapist.

Hermen is not for emergencies. Do not use Hermen if you are in danger. Hermen always shows the user how to contact a human therapist.

## 5 Procedure: prepare for a therapy session

1. Open the Hermen web page.
2. Choose Hermen or Herma as your guide.
3. Sign in with Masky, or continue without signing in.
4. Type what is on your mind. Write it in your own words.
5. Read the reflection that Hermen gives you.
6. Tell Hermen if the reflection is wrong. Correct it.
7. Read the words that Hermen flags as easy to misread.
8. Tell Hermen which meaning is closer to what you mean.
9. Ask Hermen to help you say the hardest thing. Choose one of the three versions.
10. Ask Hermen to make your prep card.
11. Review the prep card. Change any part that is not correct.
12. Print the prep card, copy it, or save it on your phone.
13. Bring the prep card to your therapy session. Show it to your therapist.
14. Ask your therapist: "Can you tell me what you heard?"

## 6 Safety

If you mention suicide, self-harm, or danger, Hermen stops the exercise. Hermen shows crisis lines. Contact a crisis line now.

- United States: call or text 988.
- United Kingdom and Ireland: call 116 123 (Samaritans).
- Anywhere: go to findahelpline.com.
- Any country: call your local emergency number.

The crisis lines are fixed in the web page. The AI model does not decide them.

## 7 Privacy

Hermen does not store chats. The chat stays in your browser tab. When you close the tab, the chat is deleted.

Your words go to the AI model only to write the replies.

Saving is your choice. A signed-in user can save a prep card. Hermen saves only the prep card. Hermen does not save the chat. You can delete a saved card at any time.

Hermen does not learn your real name or email. You sign in with a Masky avatar.
