Hermen

Get ready to be understood.

Hermen helps you get ready for therapy. Talk it through first, in private, with a Masky avatar guide. Then say what you mean in the room, and check that your therapist heard it.

The problem

Therapy's overlooked barrier is being misunderstood.

Therapy is hard to start: cost, finding someone, stigma, time. One reason gets missed: being misunderstood, and the fear of it.

Hermeneutics, in plain words

The study of how people understand each other: the gap between what one person says and what the other hears. The word comes from Hermes, the Greek messenger god who carried words between gods and humans. Something could get lost on the way.

Where that gap hurts most

  • You arrive vulnerable, to sit with a stranger.
  • You are on a clock: about 50 minutes.
  • You are saying things you may never have said out loud.
  • Vulnerable, we lose our words. We say something close, but not quite it.
"I'm just tired of everything."
Burned out at work? Done with a relationship? Something darker? Each reading leads to a very different session, and you may never learn which one your therapist heard.
Why we built it
"Before I went to therapy, I did a lot of work on voicing what was going on with me. It had a great impact on how I could talk with my therapist. I would even phrase the same thing in different ways, to see if my therapist was understanding me."The founder
What Hermen does

One session, about 10 minutes.

  1. Pick your guideHermen or Herma. Sign in with Masky as an avatar: no real name, no email.
  2. Say it messyHowever it comes out is fine. One gentle question at a time.
  3. "Here's what I heard"Your guide reflects it back and flags words a therapist could misread. You correct it. Say, reflect, correct.
  4. Say it three waysPlain, gentle, and with an example, for the hardest thing.
  5. Take your prep cardOne page to print, copy, or keep on your phone.
My prep card

What I most want my therapist to understand

I'm not just stressed. I feel flat and worn out most days, and it's been months.

The hardest thing, three ways

  1. I think I'm more than tired. I think I'm low.
  2. It's hard to explain, but everything feels heavy lately.
  3. Last Sunday I cancelled plans I'd been looking forward to, and felt nothing.

Words I use that might be misread

"Tired of everything" means worn out and flat. It does not mean I want to hurt myself.

What I want from this session

Just be heard first, then work toward a plan.

Check-back question

"Can you tell me what you heard?"

Meet your guides

Hermen and Herma.

Same brain, same rules, different face and voice. You pick who you would rather open up to. Plain words, short replies, one question at a time. They never diagnose, and they always point you back to your human therapist.

Hermen

From Hermes and hermeneutics: the messenger, the interpreter. A friend who listens well. Not a doctor.

Herma

A herma was a stone marker at Greek crossroads and doorways, a guardian of thresholds. She waits with you outside the therapy door.

Safety and privacy

Non-negotiable.

  • Hermen is not a therapist and is not for emergencies. It says so on every screen.
  • If you mention suicide, self-harm, or danger, Hermen stops the exercise, says why kindly, and shows crisis lines. This is hard-coded in the page. The AI does not get to decide it.
  • We don't save your conversation. It lives in your browser tab. Close the tab and it's gone.
  • Saving is your choice. Only a prep card is stored, never the chat, under your Masky avatar. Delete it anytime.
  • You sign in as an avatar, so we never learn your real name or email.
In crisis? 988 (US) · 116 123 Samaritans (UK and Ireland) · findahelpline.com (anywhere) · or your local emergency number.
